10 Ways to Cut Your Electricity Bill

Energy by Jerone L

Switching to LED bulbs and unplugging idle chargers is the easiest place to begin.

Next, use natural light during the day, set your AC to 25°C or above, and consider a rooftop solar panel. Small changes add up to a noticeably lower bill and a smaller carbon footprint.

Zero-Waste Kitchen for Beginners

Waste by Jerone L

Start by replacing single-use plastic with steel containers and cloth bags.

Compost vegetable peels in a small bin, buy loose grains instead of packets, and carry your own bottle. Within a month your dustbin will fill up much more slowly.

Eating Local, Eating Seasonal

Food by Jerone L

Food that travels fewer kilometres leaves behind far fewer emissions.

Visit your nearby farmers' market, choose seasonal vegetables and millets, and try a meat-free day each week. It is good for your health, your wallet and the planet.
